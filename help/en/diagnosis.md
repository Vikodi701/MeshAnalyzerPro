# Diagnosis and Recommendations

This page analyzes the loaded mesh data and provides readable diagnostics and maintenance recommendations.

## What does it show?

- Smart diagnostic summary
- Mechanical diagnostic report
- Bed tilt, surface irregularity, and possible mechanical issues
- Practical calibration or maintenance suggestions

## How to use it

1. Open a mesh file.
2. Go to **Diagnosis and Recommendations** from the sidebar.
3. Review the smart diagnostic and mechanical diagnostic sections.

## Tips

- Large total deviation may indicate bed tilt or mechanical adjustment issues.
- High RMS may indicate that the surface is generally uneven.
- Review the diagnosis together with the Heatmap, Topography, and Analysis pages.
